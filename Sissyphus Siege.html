PURGATORY // VERSION 1

THE SETTING

You are still alive. Purgatory wants to correct that.

You wake beneath a dead moon on an endless road between death and release. Far ahead stands The Gate — the only visible way out — but every time you approach it, the landscape stretches and the Gate retreats deeper into the dark.

The realm builds enemies from corrupted memories, unfinished souls, animal instinct, rusted machinery and human remains. Each descent becomes less recognizable, more hostile, and more desperate. Your only objective is brutally simple: keep moving toward the Gate.

Survive waves, learn the creatures, chain attacks, exploit headshots, and create enough distance to regenerate before Purgatory closes around you again.

The Gate: Kill every creature on a depth to unseal the Gate. Walk through it to descend into a harder layer of Purgatory; enemy numbers, durability, speed and species variety keep increasing.
